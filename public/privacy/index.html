THE COMMISSIONER INVITATIONAL

Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026

The short version

The Commissioner Invitational (tcigolfgame.com) is a free fan-made game. There are no accounts and no sign-ups, and we don't ask for your name or email to play. Your game progress stays in your own browser. We use Google Analytics to count visits and see how the game is used. If we show ads in the future, ad partners such as Google may use cookies, as described below.

What we collect

  • Information stored on your device. Your teams, matchups, handicap edits and progress are saved in your browser's local storage so you can pick up where you left off. This stays on your device. We do not receive it. Clearing your browser data deletes it.
  • Usage information through Google Analytics. We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how people use the game, such as which screens are viewed, how many people start and finish a tournament, and how long visits last. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies and receives information such as your approximate location (derived from your IP address), device and browser type, and the pages you view. We do not use it to identify you personally.
  • Hosting and security logs. The site is hosted by Cloudflare, which processes technical data such as your IP address in order to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.
  • Fonts. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google's servers to download them.
  • Messages you send us. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include, and we use it only to respond.

Cookies and advertising

Cookies are small files stored by your browser. Google Analytics uses them to tell visits apart. We may in the future display ads on this site through Google AdSense or a similar ad network. If we do, third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can learn more about how Google uses data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on, and you can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The game will still work.

How we use information

We use the information above to run and improve the game, understand how many people use it and which features matter, keep the site secure, and, if we show ads, to support the cost of running it. We do not sell personal information, and we do not collect information to build profiles of individual players.

Sharing

We share information only with the service providers described above (Google for analytics, fonts and any future advertising, and Cloudflare for hosting) and where required by law.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example, California or the European Economic Area), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to certain processing. Because we don't run accounts and don't collect your name or contact details to play, there is generally nothing for us to look up, but you can contact us with any request and we will do our best to help. You can delete your saved game at any time by clearing your browser's site data for tcigolfgame.com.

About this site

The Commissioner Invitational is an independent, fan-made game. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the Internet Invitational, Bob Does Sports, YouTube Golf, or any of the players, creators or companies mentioned in the game. Simulated results are for entertainment only and do not represent what actually happened or would happen in any real event.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed.
